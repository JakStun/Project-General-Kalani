# Cost of this project:

- Servos: 26 e (MG90S 180, MG996 180)
- MIC: 18 e (I bought second one for stereo and as a backup)
- Wiring: ~ 5 e
- Sensors: 30 e (LD2410, LD2410C, LD2450) (one LD2410C sensor self-combusted for some reason, had to buy new one...)
- RPi Camera V2: 16.5 e (testing human recognition)
- Adafruit 4 Ω 3 W Speakers - 10 e
- MAX98357 I2S mono amplifier 3W - 10 e (2x)
- LEDS: ~ 20 e (various types)

- TOTAL COST: 135.5 euros

- price is even higher though, these are just things I needed to buy.
